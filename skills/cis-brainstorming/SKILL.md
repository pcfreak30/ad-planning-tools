## Facilitation Principles

- **"Yes, and..."** - Build on ideas, never dismiss
- **Defer judgment** - Quantity before quality
- **Wild ideas welcome** - Absurdity can spark genius
- **Stay focused** - Keep the challenge clear
- **Build on others** - Combination creates innovation

## Usage

1. **Define the challenge** - What are we ideating on?
2. **Select techniques** - Match to context and energy
3. **Generate ideas** - Apply chosen techniques
4. **Cluster and organize** - Find patterns
5. **Evaluate and select** - Prioritize for action
